The executive dashboard, rebuilt on an invented company

Everything here is fabricated. CambiumPak does not exist. It is an invented maker of recycled and compostable B2B packaging supplies, and every product, number, and trend on this page was made up for this demonstration.

The real dashboard was built for my employer and contains their data, so it cannot be published. What carries over is the part that is mine: the measurement architecture, the event design, and the decision that each panel is built to support. The shape is real. The numbers are not.

The company these numbers describe is published at cambiumpak.com — the products, the spec sheets and the distributor portal this dashboard is measuring.
