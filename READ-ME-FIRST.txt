WHAT CHANGED

1. fonts/ - styles.css loads three woff2 files from fonts/, but in the repo
   they sat loose at the root, so every one 404'd in production and the live
   site rendered in a system fallback font. This is the most visible fix.

2. drive-the-505/ - work-drive-505.html linked to drive-the-505/index.html,
   which did not exist; only the zip was in the repo. Now the app runs at
   yoursite.com/drive-the-505/.

3. Card previews recomposed. Both were 980x660 images squeezed into a
   467x210 well by object-fit:cover, which centred the crop and sliced the
   phones through the middle of their content. Both are now cropped to the
   card's aspect ratio with the ivory margin trimmed, so the screens run
   edge to edge and bleed off the bottom - a deliberate crop, not an
   accidental one.

4. proto/bookspot-app.html - a clickable walkthrough built from the eight
   hi-fi London screens already in assets/screens26/. The whole designed
   flow is wired: splash, map, list, filter, spot detail, walking
   directions, the spotted stamp, the shelf, plus the bottom tab bar.
   There is a "show tap targets" toggle, back, restart, keyboard support,
   and a flow trail down the side. Every hotspot either navigates or tells
   you why it does not - no dead ends, and no pretending something is built
   when it is not.

5. proto/studiokeeper.html - the dashboard rebuilt from the case study's
   own structure. Four time windows (30 days, YTD, 2025, all time), revenue
   stacked by channel with hover tooltips and a table view, booked versus
   kept per channel, insights that carry their own caveats, orders that
   open for line items, a restock radar whose reorder button files a task,
   and the tax envelope. Sample data, labelled as sample on the page.

6. index.html + work-studio.html - the last "Desert Palette" references are
   now StudioKeeper, and the prototype button points at proto/studiokeeper.html.

Every href, src and CSS url() in the repo now resolves. Nothing 404s.
