WHAT CHANGED

1. fonts/ - styles.css loads the three woff2 files from fonts/, but in the
   repo they sit loose at the root. Every one was 404ing in production, so
   the live site has been rendering in a system fallback font, not Space
   Grotesk / IBM Plex Mono. This is the fix that will visibly change the
   site the most.

2. drive-the-505/ - work-drive-505.html links to drive-the-505/index.html,
   which did not exist; only the zip was in the repo. Unpacked it, so the
   app now runs at yoursite.com/drive-the-505/.

3. The two card previews, recomposed. Both were 980x660 images being
   squeezed into a 467x210 well by object-fit:cover, which centred the crop
   and sliced the phones through the middle of their content. Both are now
   cropped to the card's own aspect ratio with the ivory margin trimmed, so
   the screens run edge to edge and bleed off the bottom - a deliberate
   crop rather than an accidental one. object-position:top holds the bleed
   at the bottom at any card width.

4. index.html - the work card said "Desert Palette." Now StudioKeeper.

5. work-studio.html - "Desert Palette" replaced in the page title, the OG
   tag, the h1, and the prototype label; the demo button now points at
   proto/studiokeeper.html.
