WHAT CHANGED

1. fonts/ - styles.css loads three woff2 files from fonts/, but in the repo
   they sat loose at the root, so every one 404'd in production and the live
   site rendered in a system fallback font. Most visible fix on the list.

2. drive-the-505/ - work-drive-505.html linked to drive-the-505/index.html,
   which did not exist; only the zip was in the repo. The app now runs at
   yoursite.com/drive-the-505/.

3. Choosing a Car is now a matcher, not a list of your criteria. Six
   questions - budget, the road you dread most, size, where the car parks,
   which features would settle your nerves, how you feel about buying used
   - then a ranked shortlist of three from a twelve-car catalogue. Each pick
   shows a fit score, the specific reasons it matched YOUR answers, the
   caveats counting against it, and what to watch out for on that model.
   It is honest where it matters: under $10k it says out loud that you are
   usually choosing between modern safety tech and low miles; anything that
   depends on trim or model year says so instead of being claimed; the cars
   that ranked lower are still listed as good cars. Your original "what to
   look for" checklist stays, reframed as the reasoning behind the picks.
   Built on the app's existing quiz styling, so it looks native.

4. Card previews recomposed. Both were 980x660 images squeezed into a
   467x210 well by object-fit:cover, which centred the crop and sliced the
   phones through the middle of their content. Both are now cropped to the
   card's aspect ratio with the ivory margin trimmed, so the screens run
   edge to edge and bleed off the bottom.

5. proto/bookspot-app.html - a clickable walkthrough built from the eight
   hi-fi London screens already in assets/screens26/. The whole designed
   flow: splash, map, list, filter, spot detail, walking directions, the
   spotted stamp, the shelf, and the bottom tab bar. "Show tap targets"
   toggle, back, restart, keyboard support, flow trail. Every hotspot either
   navigates or tells you why it does not.

6. proto/studiokeeper.html - the dashboard rebuilt from the case study's own
   structure. Four time windows, revenue stacked by channel with hover
   tooltips and a table view, booked versus kept per channel, insights that
   carry their own caveats, orders that open for line items, a restock radar
   whose reorder button files a task, the tax envelope. Sample data,
   labelled as sample on the page.

7. index.html + work-studio.html - the last "Desert Palette" references are
   now StudioKeeper, and the prototype button points at proto/studiokeeper.html.

Every href, src and CSS url() in the repo resolves. Nothing 404s.
