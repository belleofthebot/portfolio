IF fonts/ FAILS AGAIN

  The three woff2 files are already live at the repo root — that is where they
  have always been. So instead of uploading, you can edit styles.css on GitHub
  and delete "fonts/" from the three url() calls on lines 8, 9 and 10:
      url('fonts/space-grotesk-latin-400-normal.woff2')
  becomes
      url('space-grotesk-latin-400-normal.woff2')
  Three deletions and the typography works. If you do that, leave the woff2
  files at the repo root alone in any future cleanup — they become load-bearing.
