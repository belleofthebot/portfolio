WHAT CHANGED

FIXES

1. fonts/ — styles.css loads three woff2 files from fonts/, but in the repo
   they sat loose at the root, so every one 404'd in production and the live
   site rendered in a system fallback font. The most visible fix on this list.

2. drive-the-505/ — work-drive-505.html linked to drive-the-505/index.html,
   which did not exist; only the zip was in the repo. The app now runs at
   yoursite.com/drive-the-505/.

3. drive-the-505-preview.jpg — index.html pointed at it and it did not exist,
   so that work card showed a broken image.

4. Both card previews recomposed. They were 980x660 images squeezed into a
   467x210 well by object-fit:cover, which centred the crop and sliced the
   phones through the middle of their content. Now cropped to the card's own
   aspect ratio with the ivory margin trimmed, so the screens run edge to edge
   and bleed off the bottom — a deliberate crop rather than an accidental one.

5. assets/app/logo-white.png — the Bookspot logo had a cream ground baked into
   the JPG, showing as a pale square on the sage splash. Knocked out to
   transparency.

PROTOTYPES — both are ports of YOUR files, not reconstructions

6. proto/bookspot-app.html — your 2026 prototype from Claude Design, ported to
   plain HTML. Fifteen London spots, splash, city picker, the four-step tour,
   map and list views, quick genre chips, spot detail with save/unsave, walking
   directions, the stamp screen with running progress, the shelf holding stamps
   and saved spots, the full filter (genre, author, era, walking distance) with
   a live count, More and About. Every bit of state carries through the session.

7. proto/studiokeeper.html — your Desert Palette dashboard, ported and renamed
   StudioKeeper. All seven tabs, four time windows, the three-ways-in add flow,
   aging tasks, orders that open for line items, per-type product filtering, the
   restock radar, the quarterly filing table. SKUs renamed DP- to SK-; gallery
   and wholesale venue names scrubbed to match your red line about client data.
   This replaces the version I had built from the case study — yours is better.

   Why both had to be ported: the .dc.html format needs support.js plus the
   _ds/belleofthebot-design-system-... bundle to run. These ports need nothing,
   so they work on your own domain permanently.

CASE STUDY

8. work-studio.html now matches your newer version. Added: the six-source chaos

   work-bookspot.html already matched: it has the 2026 facelift section, the

DRIVE THE 505

9.  Choosing a Car is a matcher now. Six questions — budget, the road you dread,
    size, where the car parks, which features would settle your nerves, how you
    feel about buying used — then a ranked shortlist of three from a twelve-car
    catalogue, each with a fit score, the reasons it matched your answers, the
    caveats against it, and what to watch for on that model. It says out loud
    that under $10k you are usually choosing between modern safety tech and low
    miles, and anything trim- or year-dependent says so. The "What to look for"
    criteria table is removed, as you asked.

10. Reordered: Car Knowledge, Driving Theory, NM Road Rules and ABQ Geography
    come first; Choosing a Car, Buying a Car and First Month follow, so the
    checklists sit at the end.

11. Every emoji replaced with hand-drawn inline SVG — nav, home cards, module
    cards, the Study / Flashcards / Quiz tabs, both map switches. They use
    currentColor so they take the app's gold automatically.

Every href, src and CSS url() in the repo resolves. Nothing 404s.
