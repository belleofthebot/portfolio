about

How I got here

I spent the first decade of my career making things: illustration, design, video, teaching graduate students how to tell a story in pictures. Then I got tired of handing work over and never finding out whether it moved anything.

So I learned the other half. Now I build the tracking, the dashboards and the systems that answer the question, and I still make the thing itself. That combination is unusual. Most teams hire a creative and an analyst and hope they talk to each other.

I was hired in March 2026 into a production design role: Adobe Creative Cloud, sell sheets, social graphics, motion. Within four months the work had expanded into website ownership, analytics engineering and shipping internal software, because those things needed doing and there was nobody else to do them. All of the software on this site was built by someone with no formal development background, no developer, and no budget beyond a subscription, alongside a full design workload. That trajectory is the story: the role outgrew its job description in a single quarter.

None of it happened in isolation. The tracking build ran through IT on account access and through the agency on implementation. The website work ran on a contract boundary agreed with the vendor. The tools shipped with written procedures so somebody other than me can run them.

I am based in Albuquerque, work well remotely, and am looking for marketing operations, marketing analytics or marketing technology roles where this is the job rather than a thing I do after hours.
