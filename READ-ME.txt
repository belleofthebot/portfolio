IF THE FONTS FOLDER FAILS AGAIN — a fix that needs no file upload

The three woff2 files are ALREADY live at the repo root (that is where they have
always been). So you can just point the CSS at them:

  1. Open styles.css on GitHub, click the pencil to edit
  2. On lines 8, 9 and 10, delete "fonts/" from each url() — so
       url('fonts/space-grotesk-latin-400-normal.woff2')
     becomes
       url('space-grotesk-latin-400-normal.woff2')
  3. Commit

Three deletions, and the typography works. Note if you do this: the woff2 files
at the repo root are then load-bearing — do not delete them in any cleanup.
