WHAT EACH ONE DOES

1. styles.css — THE FONTS FIX, no folder required.
   The fonts/ folder has now failed to upload three times, while the three
   woff2 files have been sitting at the REPO ROOT the whole time, serving
   fine. So each @font-face now lists two sources:

     src: url('fonts/space-grotesk-...woff2') format('woff2'),
          url('space-grotesk-...woff2') format('woff2');

   The browser tries the first, and falls through to the second when it
   404s. So the type starts working the moment you upload this one file,
   and it keeps working if the fonts/ folder ever does land. Nothing to
   delete, nothing to remember.

2. proto/passage-app.html — two fixes.

   The order bug you spotted. ORDER was built with Object.keys(), and
   JavaScript quietly hoists integer-like keys: '10' through '15' sorted
   ahead of '01', so the prototype opened on the meditation player and the
   jump chips started at ten. It is an explicit list now. It opens on
   Welcome and runs 01 to 15.

   The daily-needs rings, at the top of the Lucia tab. Five rings —
   medicines, meals, hygiene, movement, rest — reading 9 of 12 done,
   which is exactly what the line underneath already claimed. This is the
   one thing the 2022 home screen did better than the redesign: completion
   across categories, answered by looking instead of reading. The arcs are
   ink-toned and finished rings take the pale meadow of your existing
   done-check chip, so forest green still only ever means "you can press
   this."
