1. THE TAB BAR NOW WORKS FROM THE ICON

   You were right, and the reason is unglamorous: the wiring had landed on
   the 9px label span instead of the tab item, so only the word HOME was
   live, not the little house above it. A 31x10 target. Nobody aims at that.

   Rather than patch each one by hand, the prototype now measures its own
   targets after every screen change. Anything rendering smaller than a
   finger gets promoted to the first ancestor big enough to hit, bounded so
   nothing ends up swallowing half the screen. Thin text links like
   "Everything for Lucia" get a transparent vertical hit extender that
   leaves the layout untouched. Checked every tab on every screen that has
   one: all four navigate from the icon now.

2. THE NUDGE

   Until the first tap, a slow ring breathes around the tab bar and a small
   TAP THE TABS TO MOVE AROUND chip sits just above it. Both disappear the
   moment you interact and never come back in that session, and the
   animation holds still for anyone with reduced motion turned on. The line
   under the phone on the case study now says the same thing in words.

3. THE THUMBNAILS

   Diagnosed: I had been capturing each screen through the phone frame, so
   its 52px corner radius clipped the dark page background into all four
   corners of every image. That dark corner then sat inside the gallery's
   own 16px rounding, which is exactly the sliver you were seeing.

   Recaptured with the frame chrome stripped, so every screen is square
   edged and full bleed. The Passage card preview is rebuilt too: three
   panels butted edge to edge with no gaps, the same construction as the
   Bookspot card.

4. STILL CARRYING THE TWO STRAYS

   styles.css — each @font-face lists fonts/<file>.woff2 first and
   <file>.woff2 second, so the browser falls through to the copies already
   at your repo root. Upload this one file and the typography starts
   working, no folder needed.
