Choosing a Car
SIX QUESTIONS, THEN CARS THAT SUIT A NERVOUS DRIVER
Find your shortlist
Six questions, about a minute

There is no single right first car. There is a right one for your budget, your streets and the things that make you nervous. Answer these and you get a shortlist you can actually go and drive, with the reason each one is on it.

New, used, or certified pre owned? New costs the most and depreciates fastest. Used is the best value but needs the inspection. Certified pre owned sits in between: dealer inspected, still under a warranty, and priced above a private sale. For a first car, used or certified pre owned with modern safety tech beats new and bare.

Test drive it properly. Fifteen minutes in a parking lot tells you nothing. Drive it on a road you find hard. Merge onto a highway. Reverse into a space. Park it. If a car makes a road you fear feel manageable, that is the car.

You are not doing this alone. The right car is helping.

Two things ride with you the whole month: your driver assist features are watching the road too (adaptive cruise, lane keep, automatic braking), and there is no such thing as too slow or too cautious. Right lane, easy pace, pull over any time to reset.
