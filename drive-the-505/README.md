# Drive the 505

A learning app for nervous drivers in Albuquerque. Choosing and buying a car,
general car knowledge, driving theory, New Mexico road rules, a first month plan,
and an interactive map of the city grid, with flashcards and quizzes throughout.

## Contents
- `index.html` — the entire app, self contained. No build step, no dependencies.
- `icon-512.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png` — app icons.

## Deploying
Drag this folder onto vercel.com/new or app.netlify.com/drop. There is nothing to build.
